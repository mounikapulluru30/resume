## 📊 Agile Delivery Focus

I use Agile metrics as **transparency and learning tools**, rather than as targets for teams.

Areas of focus include:

* Sprint Goal achievement
* Velocity trends
* Burndown / Burnup
* Work in Progress
* Defect trends
* Escaped defects
* Rework
* Blockers and impediments
* Dependencies
* Cycle time
* Continuous improvement actions

> **Principle:** Metrics should help teams inspect their situation and adapt — not become targets that drive unhealthy behaviour.
